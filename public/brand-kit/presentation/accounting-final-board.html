Final design

Trace Loop

A clear loop leaves a visible point of provenance.

  • The open loop signals traceable work without drawing a literal ledger.
  • The separate point gives the mark a distinct entry cue at small sizes.
  • Indigo and Teal match the approved workspace direction; a mint point preserves contrast in reverse.
